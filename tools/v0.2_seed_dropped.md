# v0.2 Bulk-Ingest Seed — Drop List

**Stage 20.6.** Tools and URLs dropped from the v0.2 bulk crawl after a
pre-crawl probe (this file) or after the actual crawl in Stage 20.8.
[tools/v0.2_seed.json](v0.2_seed.json) is the source of truth for the
target list; this file is the running record of what we removed and why.

## Pre-crawl probe (Stage 20.6 — JS-rendered SPA detection)

For each of the 36 KEEP tools in
[tools/v0.2_seed_licenses.md](v0.2_seed_licenses.md), one `GET` was issued
against the seed URL with the bulk-ingest `User-Agent`, the response was
HTML-sanitized via the same `_sanitize_html_to_text` the docs ingestion
service uses, and the resulting character count was recorded. A response
under ~500 chars suggests SPA-rendered content that `httpx` can't see and
would land an empty claim in the store.

**Result: zero hard SPA drops.** 35 of 36 URLs returned substantive
text. One was flagged for review:

| tool_id | host | sanitized chars | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| cargo | doc.rust-lang.org | 367 | KEEP — this is a thin index page (table-of-contents-only), not SPA. The crawl will produce a sparse claim for this URL; richer coverage of individual `cargo build`/`cargo test`/etc. pages is a v0.2.x follow-up. |

All 12 tools deferred at Stage 20.3 (license drops + REVIEW) remain
deferred; they are tracked in [tools/v0.2_seed_licenses.md](v0.2_seed_licenses.md)
and were not probed.

## Post-crawl drops (Stage 20.8 — appended after the real ingest)

The first Stage 20.8 crawl produced 32/36 successes. Four failures
surfaced; three were fixed in-place (max_bytes bump 1 → 4 MB,
relative-redirect resolution) and recovered cleanly on `--resume`. One
remains dropped:

| tool_id | url | failure | disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| gcloud | https://cloud.google.com/sdk/gcloud/reference | Docs fetch exceeded max_bytes=4194304 | DROP from v0.2 — Google's single-page reference index for the entire `gcloud` surface is >4 MB raw HTML. Bumping the default further weakens the protection layer for every other tool. The right v0.2.x fix is to replace this URL with a handful of per-command sub-pages (e.g. `/sdk/gcloud/reference/compute/instances/list`) rather than the monolithic index. |

**Final v0.2 crawl result: 35 of 36 KEEP tools ingested successfully.**
Combined with the 5 Stage 0 curated tools (docker, git, github-cli,
vercel-cli, openai-api), the v0.2 graph holds **82 claims across 40
distinct tools**, comfortably above the Stage 20 DoD floor of 35
distinct tools.

### Two bugs surfaced and fixed during the crawl

1. **`default_max_bytes` was too tight** — bumped from 1 MB → 4 MB in
   `contracts/docs_ingestion_sources.v1.json` (+ lockstep mirror).
   Recovered awk + supabase-cli.
2. **Relative redirects raised "Only https:// URLs allowed; got scheme ''"** —
   `_fetch` now resolves `Location: /relative` against the current URL
   via `urllib.parse.urljoin` before the next SSRF re-validation. Added
   regression test `test_relative_redirect_is_resolved_against_current_url`.
   Recovered imagemagick.
